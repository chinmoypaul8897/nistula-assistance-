Common guest questions, answered briefly.

**How do I book?** Booking happens on the website — pick your dates on the villa page and it re-quotes live before any hold. A reservation is confirmed once the advance deposit is received and a written confirmation is sent.

**Can I hold a room over WhatsApp?** No — the website is the booking desk; share the link and the guest books there. We never take payment in chat.

**What does the tariff include?** Accommodation only, unless meals, an airport transfer, a chef or other add-ons are listed in the confirmation. Taxes and gateway fees apply where relevant.

**Is breakfast included?** The room rate is accommodation only unless breakfast is specifically listed in the booking — the team can confirm meal options.

**Is housekeeping included?** Yes — on the property's schedule. Linen and towel changes, deep cleaning and extra housekeeping follow the operating policy and availability.

**How do I report a maintenance issue?** Tell the team as soon as you notice it and we'll attend to it as quickly as we can.

**Will Wi-Fi, power and the pool always be available?** They're subject to operational availability; we make every effort to resolve issues, though power cuts, outages and weather are beyond our control.

**Can you arrange transport, a chef or experiences?** Yes — we're glad to help coordinate airport transfers, taxis, self-drive or chauffeur cars, a private chef and other experiences through trusted partners; charges and availability are confirmed in advance.

**How do I find the property on arrival?** Share your expected arrival time and we'll send an accurate location pin and arrival guidance; gated communities may need a quick security check.

**Can I change my dates?** Date changes are subject to availability, any tariff difference and approval, so they can't be guaranteed — but we'll always try to help.

**What happens if something is damaged?** Please treat the home with care and tell the team about any breakage right away; damage, missing items or excessive cleaning may be charged, usually against the security deposit.

**Can I bring my own cook or vendors?** Outside cooks, decorators, photographers or event staff aren't permitted without prior approval — but we're glad to arrange trusted partners for you.
